BlazePay⚡

Send Bitcoin Cash with a link.

No account. No backend. No custody. The money moves on-chain — the link is just a URL.

Create a link → share it → get paid in BCH.

Problem

Getting paid in crypto is still too hard

  • To receive BCH today you must explain wallets, addresses, and copy-paste a 42-character string — and hope nothing gets mistyped.
  • Payment apps solve this, but they are custodial: they hold the money, take a cut, and need an account.
  • Small sellers, streamers, and fundraisers need the simplest possible thing: a link they can paste anywhere.

Solution

A payment link, not a payment app

  • One page. Fill in amount + message, get a shareable link and QR code.
  • Opening the link shows exactly what you pay, to whom, and why.
  • The message rides on-chain as OP_RETURN — the payment itself carries the receipt.
  • A live feed shows the latest payments to the recipient, read straight from the chain.

Demo — 2 minutes

Try it right now, for free

  1. Testnet first (free): open the demo, press Get free testnet BCH — the app makes a throwaway test wallet and funds it from the faucet.
  2. Create a link: amount 0.001 tBCH + a message → copy the link or scan the QR.
  3. Pay it: open the link, press Pay now — a real testnet transaction, with a txid and explorer link.
  4. Mainnet: flip the toggle, scan the WizardConnect QR with Paytaca / Selene / Cashonize, sign in your wallet. Same code, real BCH.

Technology

Built on BCH-native tooling

mainnet-js v3

Wallets, transaction building, Electrum reads (UTXOs, balances, history). One code path for testnet and mainnet — only the network flips.

WizardConnect

QR handshake over an encrypted Nostr relay. The wallet shares its xpub; BlazePay derives the address and requests a signature. Keys never leave the wallet.

OP_RETURN memo

Every payment can carry a message on-chain — the payment and its purpose are one transaction.

CashTokens

The project address is token-aware; EMBER2026 donations fund Community Choice voting.

Static site, zero backend, zero custody. Hosting: GitHub Pages (free).

Why BCH

Why this only works on Bitcoin Cash

  • Fees are sub-cent — a payment link for a coffee makes sense; on fee-heavy chains it does not.
  • Fast, reliable confirmation — the payer sees the receipt in seconds, not after a queue.
  • OP_RETURN + CashTokens are native — memos and token donations need no smart contract.
  • UTXO + Electrum — balances and payment history are readable by any client, no indexer or API key.

Business & next steps

From demo to a public good

  • Who pays: nobody has to. The tool is free; the market is every small seller, creator, and fundraiser who wants BCH without an app.
  • Path to revenue (optional): an optional 0.1% convenience output, off by default, for hosted/white-label versions.
  • Next: CashTokens tipping (pay in any token), recurring links, and a no-code embed for shops.
